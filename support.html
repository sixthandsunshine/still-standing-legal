Quick help

  • Daily reminders: open the app → Settings → turn on the reminder and pick your time.
  • Text size: Settings → Text size (Small, Default, Large, or Larger).
  • Favorites: tap the heart on any card to save it; find saved items in Favorites.
  • Still Standing Plus: unlocks the full library, "What do you need today?", a private journal, and extra themes. Manage or restore a purchase from the Plus tab or your app store account.
  • Restore a purchase: open the Plus tab (or the paywall) → Restore purchases.
  • Your privacy: the app works with no account, and your journal, favorites, and settings stay on your device.

A note on hard days

Still Standing offers encouragement, but it is not medical or mental-health care. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please reach out for help right now. In the U.S., call or text 988 (the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), available 24/7. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.
